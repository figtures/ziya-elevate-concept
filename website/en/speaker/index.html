01

Engineering Success

45 / 60 / 90 minutes · Format agreed for your event

For: Company kickoffs, entrepreneur events and teams turning goals into action.

The question: Can success be designed?

What participants leave with: The Ben Yasası principle, moving from complaint to system building, and a clear starting framework for building a first working system.

Discuss this keynote
02

Bulletproof Mindset

45 / 60 / 90 minutes · Format agreed for your event

For: Teams facing change, sales pressure, rejection or uncertainty.

The question: What can we choose when we cannot choose what happens?

What participants leave with: Recognising the inner saboteur, using the language of NOT YET–DESPITE–POSSIBLE, and practising a 90-second pause–notice–choose response.

Discuss this keynote
03

The Engineering of Sales

45 / 60 / 90 minutes · Format agreed for your event

For: B2B teams, sales conferences and dealer meetings.

The question: Are we presenting a product or diagnosing a problem?

What participants leave with: A practical framework for diagnosis, value, evidence, decisions and follow-through.

Discuss this keynote
04

Leadership: Multiplying Impact

45 / 60 / 90 minutes · Format agreed for your event

For: Leadership teams, management programmes and family businesses.

The question: What continues to work when the leader steps back?

What participants leave with: A way to develop other leaders through observation, demonstration, development, stepping back and multiplication.

Discuss this keynote

Meet Ziya

Experience,
made useful.

Ziya Şakir Yılmaz is a Success Engineer, author and keynote speaker working across success, mindset, sales and leadership. Drawing on international business experience and a personal journey from rural Türkiye, he turns ideas into practical models. His goal: audiences leave with a framework and three clear next steps to apply.
